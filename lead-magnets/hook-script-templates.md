# 15 Proven Hook Script Templates
## Copy-Paste Formulas to Stop the Scroll in 2 Seconds

---

## HOW TO USE THESE TEMPLATES

1. **Choose a template** that fits your content type
2. **Fill in the blanks** with your specific topic
3. **Say it with energy** - delivery matters as much as words
4. **Show, don't just tell** - add visuals that match the hook
5. **Test different hooks** for the same content to see what performs

## TEMPLATE #1: THE "STOP SCROLLING IF" HOOK

**Formula:**
> "Stop scrolling if you [specific situation/desire]"

**Examples:**
- "Stop scrolling if you want to make money while you sleep"
- "Stop scrolling if you've been struggling with [problem]"
- "Stop scrolling if you're a [target audience] who wants [result]"

**Fill-in-the-blank:**
> "Stop scrolling if you ________________________________"

**Why it works:** Creates immediate relevance. Viewers self-select based on their situation.

**Best for:** Educational content, tutorials, tips, product reveals

## TEMPLATE #7: THE BEFORE/AFTER HOOK

**Formula:**

**Examples:**

- "I tried [product/method] for 30 days. Here's what happened."

**Fill-in-the-blank:**

**Best for:** Fitness, skincare, skills, room makeovers, any transformation content

- "Unpopular opinion: Going to gym every day is actually bad for you"
- "Unpopular opinion: [Common advice] is completely wrong"

## BONUS: HOOK COMBINATIONS

Mix and match elements for unique hooks:

- **Question + Number:** "Why do 90% of businesses fail? Here are 3 reasons..."
- **POV + Story:** "POV: You're me when I discovered this secret..."
- **Controversy + Tutorial:** "Unpopular opinion: Here's the REAL way to..."
- **Stop Scrolling + Before/After:** "Stop scrolling if you want skin like this..."

## THE HOOK TESTING FRAMEWORK

For maximum results, create **3-5 different hooks** for each piece of content:

| Version | Hook Type | Angle |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| A | Question | Curiosity |
| B | POV | Relatability |
| C | Number | Authority |
| D | Story | Emotion |
| E | Controversy | Engagement |

Post on different days or platforms to test which performs best. Double down on what works.

## DELIVERY TIPS

✅ **DO:**
- Start speaking immediately (no dead air)
- Maintain eye contact with the lens
- Use hand gestures for emphasis
- Vary your tone and pace
- Show energy and enthusiasm
- Match your facial expression to the hook

❌ **DON'T:**
- Start with "Hey guys" or "So..."
- Look away from camera at the start
- Speak in monotone
- Sound scripted or robotic
- Wait more than 0.5 seconds to speak
