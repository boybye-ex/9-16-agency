## PRE-PRODUCTION CHECKLIST

### Lighting Setup
- [ ] Position yourself facing a window for natural light (best: golden hour or overcast day)
- [ ] If using artificial lights, place them at 45-degree angles to avoid harsh shadows
- [ ] Avoid overhead lighting that creates unflattering shadows under eyes
- [ ] Check for mixed lighting (warm + cool) that causes color issues
- [ ] Use a ring light for even, flattering face lighting (R300-R800 budget option)
- [ ] Ensure no backlight that silhouettes your subject

### Audio Quality
- [ ] Record in a quiet space (no AC, fans, or traffic noise)
- [ ] Use a lapel mic or phone's earphone mic for better audio (R150-R400)
- [ ] Test audio levels before recording full take
- [ ] Speak clearly and slightly louder than normal conversation
- [ ] If using music, ensure it doesn't overpower your voice
- [ ] Record a 5-second silence for noise reference in editing

### Framing & Composition
- [ ] Phone held vertically (9:16 aspect ratio)
- [ ] Subject centered or following rule of thirds
- [ ] Leave headroom but not too much empty space above
- [ ] Keep important elements in the "safe zone" (away from edges)
- [ ] Background is clean and uncluttered
- [ ] Check for distracting elements in frame
- [ ] Eye level or slightly above for flattering angles

### Technical Settings
- [ ] Camera lens clean
- [ ] Phone storage has space for recording
- [ ] Battery above 50%
- [ ] Do Not Disturb mode enabled
- [ ] Record in highest quality available (4K if possible)
- [ ] Lock focus and exposure before recording

## THE HOOK (First 2 Seconds)

### Hook Formulas That Stop The Scroll

**The Pattern Interrupt**
- Start mid-action or mid-sentence
- Open with an unexpected visual or sound
- Break the fourth wall immediately

**The Question Hook**
- "Did you know...?"
- "Why does nobody talk about...?"
- "What if I told you...?"

**The Bold Statement**
- "This changed my life"
- "Stop doing [common thing] immediately"
- "The truth about [topic] nobody admits"

**The POV Hook**
- "POV: You just discovered..."
- "POV: Your boss when you..."
- Sets up relatable scenarios

### Hook Checklist
- [ ] First frame is visually interesting (no blank intros)
- [ ] First word spoken within 0.5 seconds
- [ ] Hook creates curiosity or emotion
- [ ] Text on screen reinforces the hook
- [ ] No logos or intros at the start
- [ ] Energy is high from frame one

## DURING FILMING

### Performance Tips
- [ ] Maintain eye contact with the camera lens
- [ ] Use hand gestures to add energy
- [ ] Vary your tone and pace
- [ ] Move closer to camera for emphasis
- [ ] Show genuine emotion/reaction
- [ ] Talk TO the viewer, not AT them

### B-Roll Checklist
- [ ] Capture multiple angles of products/actions
- [ ] Get close-up detail shots
- [ ] Film hands doing activities
- [ ] Record environmental/context shots
- [ ] Shoot more than you think you need
- [ ] Capture both fast and slow movements

---

## POST-PRODUCTION

### Editing Must-Haves
- [ ] Trim dead air at start and end
- [ ] Add captions (85% of viewers watch without sound)
- [ ] Use jump cuts to maintain pace
- [ ] Add trending or relevant audio
- [ ] Include text overlays for key points
- [ ] Color correct for consistent look
- [ ] Export in 1080x1920 (9:16) resolution

### Caption Best Practices
- [ ] Keep captions in the center-bottom safe zone
- [ ] Use high-contrast colors (white with black outline)
- [ ] Maximum 2 lines on screen at once
- [ ] Sync captions with speech (not too fast)
- [ ] Use ALL CAPS sparingly for emphasis
- [ ] Choose a bold, readable font

---

## CAPTION & HASHTAG STRATEGY

### Writing Scroll-Stopping Captions
- [ ] Lead with the most important point
- [ ] First line = hook (appears before "more")
- [ ] Include a clear CTA (comment, save, share, follow)
- [ ] Ask a question to drive engagement
- [ ] Use line breaks for readability
- [ ] Keep it under 150 characters for max visibility

### Hashtag Strategy (TikTok)
- [ ] Use 3-5 relevant hashtags
- [ ] Mix broad (#fyp) and niche (#saentrepreneur)
- [ ] Include trending hashtags when relevant
- [ ] Avoid banned or overused hashtags
- [ ] Create a branded hashtag for your content

### Hashtag Strategy (Instagram Reels)
- [ ] Use 5-15 hashtags
- [ ] Research hashtag size (avoid 10M+ only)
- [ ] Include location-based tags (#johannesburg)
- [ ] Mix hashtag sizes: small, medium, large
- [ ] Put hashtags in caption or first comment
