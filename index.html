Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

What platforms do you create content for?

We specialize in vertical 9:16 content for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels. Our content is optimized for each platform's unique algorithm and audience behavior. We also create supporting content like Stories, static posts, and carousels for Instagram and Facebook.

How long are your contracts?

We work on a month-to-month basis with no long-term lock-in contracts. We believe in earning your business every month. However, we recommend a minimum 3-month commitment to see meaningful results, as social media growth takes time to compound. Most of our clients stay with us for 12+ months.

What is the turnaround time for content?

Standard turnaround is 5-7 business days from brief approval to final delivery. For our Enterprise clients, we offer same-day turnaround for urgent requests. We also batch-create content at the start of each month based on your approved content calendar, so you always have a pipeline of ready-to-post content.

Do you provide the raw video files?

Yes! You own all the content we create for you. We deliver final edited videos in platform-optimized formats, plus the raw footage and project files upon request. This means you can repurpose content for other channels, ads, or your website.

Can I approve content before it goes live?

Absolutely. Every piece of content goes through our approval workflow before publishing. You'll receive content via a shared drive or project management tool (we use Notion), where you can review, comment, and request changes. Nothing goes live without your green light.

What if I need more content than my package includes?

No problem! Additional videos can be purchased at R800 per video for Starter clients, R650 for Growth clients, and R500 for Enterprise clients. If you consistently need more content, we'll recommend upgrading to a package that better fits your needs. We also offer custom packages for high-volume requirements.

Do you handle influencer partnerships?

Yes, influencer marketing is included in our Growth and Enterprise packages. We handle everything from identifying the right influencers for your brand, negotiating rates, briefing them on deliverables, reviewing content, and tracking performance. We have relationships with 500+ South African creators across various niches.

How do you measure success and ROI?

We track both vanity metrics (views, likes, followers) and business metrics (website traffic, leads, sales). For paid campaigns, we focus on ROAS (Return on Ad Spend), CPA (Cost Per Acquisition), and conversion rates. You'll receive detailed reports showing exactly how your investment is performing. We set clear KPIs at the start of our engagement.

What industries do you specialize in?

We work with brands across e-commerce, beauty, fashion, fitness, food & beverage, fintech, and professional services. Our sweet spot is consumer brands looking to scale through short-form video. We've also had success with B2B brands humanizing their presence on social media. If you're unsure if we're a fit, book a free audit and we'll give you an honest assessment.

How do we communicate during the project?

Communication depends on your package. Starter clients get email support and a monthly strategy call. Growth clients get a dedicated WhatsApp group with 24-hour response times and bi-weekly calls. Enterprise clients get direct access to their account manager via WhatsApp/Slack with same-day responses and weekly strategy sessions. We adapt to your preferred communication style.
